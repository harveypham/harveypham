# The Missing Invoice

<p align="right">Refined with assistance of AI tools</p>

We had a golden rule: “**Don’t lose customers’ invoices.**”  
The reasoning was obvious — losing an invoice would be catastrophic, erode trust, and damage the relationship. The rule didn’t mean never touch customer invoices; customers often asked us to clean up or adjust them. But every time we did, we walked on eggshells.

One day, an engineer accidentally deleted more than the customer requested. An invoice was gone — completely. No trace in the system, no metadata, no backup. He assumed the worst. Mentally, he braced for the blowback and prepared how to handle the fallout.

Then something unexpected happened:
**the invoice reappeared**.

The customer couldn’t find it either, so they simply uploaded it again. No complaint. No panic. No mention of the incident at all.

The golden rule still stands — it’s important for our business. But the lesson was humbling: *we expected a catastrophe that never came**. The imagined severity of the failure was far greater than the real‑world impact.

Human resilience filled the gap.
The customer solved the problem faster than we could prepare for it.

This example reminds us that even high‑stakes rules should be tested against actual human behavior. Sometimes the worst‑case scenario is not as devastating as we assume — and humans often have an innate ability to recover long before the system does.
